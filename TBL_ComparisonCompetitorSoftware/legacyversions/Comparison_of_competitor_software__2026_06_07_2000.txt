GENERAL SOFTWARE STRUCTURE:

- Provides predefined settings for six locus types: Mitogenome, Chloroplast Genome, General, cox1, 16S, and 18S.

STANDARDIZATION OF GENE NAMES:
- Standardizes sequence annotations, including gene names.
- Provides an editing window that reports annotation errors and warnings, including missing genes and non-standardized annotations. [Editing window works on mitogenome datasets only! Why?]
- Supports feature selection through the 'Names unification table'.
- Restricts extraction to features listed in the 'Names unification table' through the 'Only extract these genes' option.

EXTRACTION OF DNA SEQUENCES:
- Extracts coding sequences (CDS) by default.
- Supports recognition of non-coding regions through the 'Set NCR threshold' option, allowing users to define a minimum NCR length for subsequent extraction. [Does not work with our dataset. Why?]
- Identifies and removes duplicated sequences using the 'Highlight Identical Sequences' function.
- Imports FASTA and Word document files into the work folder. [Why not GenBank flatfiles?]
- Extracts CDS features into separate output folders containing amino acid sequences ('CDS_AA') and nucleotide sequences ('CDS_NUC').
- In the Mitogenome workflow only, generates a 'self-translated_AA' folder containing amino acid sequences translated from CDS nucleotide sequences.

ACCOUNTING FOR GENE DUPLICATION (E.G., IRs):
- Detects duplicated genes within individual records and appends copy numbers according to occurrence order.
- Supports automatic removal of duplicated genes through the 'Resolve gene duplicates' function.

ALIGNMENT OF DNA SEQUENCES:
- Performs batch conversion among FASTA, PHYLIP, PAML, AXT, and NEXUS alignment formats.
- Executes MAFFT on multiple input files simultaneously using a common parameter set. 
- Refines alignments in batch mode using MACSE.
- Optimizes ambiguously aligned regions using trimAl, HmmCleaner, or Gblocks.
- Concatenates multiple alignments and generates partition files for downstream phylogenetic analyses.

ENABLES GENE ALIGNMENT BASED ON AAs:
- Supports three MAFFT alignment modes: 
	* Normal mode: aligns input sequences directly.
	* Codon mode: translates CDS nucleotide sequences into amino acids, aligns them, and back-translates the resulting alignment into codons.
	* N2P mode: translates CDS nucleotide sequences into amino acids and outputs amino acid alignments without back-translation.

OTHER FEATURES:
- Automates phylogenetic workflows through the 'Flowchart' function.
- Integrates sequence alignment (MAFFT, MACSE), removal of poorly aligned positions and divergent regions (Gblocks, trimAl, HmmCleaner), model selection (ModelFinder, PartitionFinder), and phylogenetic tree reconstruction (MrBayes, IQ-TREE).

OGU (WU ET AL. 2024)
########################################################################

PROGRAMMING LANGUAGE:
[INFORMATION MISSING!]

GENERAL SOFTWARE STRUCTURE:
- Consists of three modules (GB2FASTA, Evaluate, and Primer) together with an automated results visualization pipeline.
- Modules can be executed independently or in combination.

STANDARDIZATION OF GENE NAMES:
- Standardizes non-standard gene names using the '-rename' option.

EXTRACTION OF DNA SEQUENCES:
- Uses the module 'GB2FASTA' for sequence retrieval, validation, and extraction.
- Extracts coding and non-coding genomic regions, including CDS, introns, and intergenic spacers.
- Extracts sequences on a per-locus basis and generates one output file per locus.
- Extracts intergenic spacers based on annotations of flanking features (i.e., even if spacers not explicitly annotated).
- Tested on multiple benchmarking datasets: GenBank records of different sizes (mostly smaller than complete genomes), plastid genomic data from the Lamiaceae, mitochondrial data from rodents, and 308 complete plastid genomes across angiosperms

ACCOUNTING FOR GENE DUPLICATION (E.G., IRs):
- Supports handling of repeat regions:
	* Retains only the longest duplicate locus by default.
	* Retains all duplicate loci with '-allow_repeat'.
	* Standardizes inverted-repeat locus names with '-allow_invert_repeat'.
- Retains only complete loci at inverted-repeat boundaries.
- Removes redundant sequences.
- Retains a single representative sequence per organism and locus by default.

ALIGNMENT OF DNA SEQUENCES:
- Uses the module 'Evaluate' for sequence alignment and sequence-variation assessment.
- Executes MAFFT v7.475 using the '–auto' option.

OTHER FEATURES:
- Automatically evaluates aligned datasets using indicators such as missing data, alignment gaps, and ambiguous bases.
- Calculates sequence-variation metrics, including stem and terminal phylogenetic diversity.
- Designs universal primers through the module 'Primer'.

PARTITIONING_EXONS_INTRONS_SPACERS (DE MESTIER ET AL. 2025)
########################################################################

PROGRAMMING LANGUAGE:
Python

GENERAL SOFTWARE STRUCTURE:
- Single script designed for extraction of loci from a single annotated genome at a time.

STANDARDIZATION OF GENE NAMES:
[INFORMATION MISSING!]

EXTRACTION OF DNA SEQUENCES:
- Extracts exons, introns, and intergenic spacers from annotated genomes.
- Validates coding sequences using start-codon, stop-codon, internal-stop-codon, and reading-frame checks.
- Searches up to 50 bp upstream and downstream for alternative start and stop codons when validation criteria are not met.
- Retains genes containing minor premature stop codons considered likely functional.
- Requires external extraction of rRNA and tRNA genes using Geneious.
- Requires manual inspection of gene synteny prior to extraction.
- Requires manual detection of genome rearrangements, inversions, and gene synteny using progressive MAUVE in Geneious.
- Requires manual post-extraction dataset curation.
- Requires manual removal of problematic genes, associated introns and spacers, rearrangement-associated spacers, putative pseudogenes, and other non-functional sequences.

ACCOUNTING FOR GENE DUPLICATION (E.G., IRs):
- Extracts loci from a single inverted-repeat region to avoid duplicate sampling.

ALIGNMENT OF DNA SEQUENCES:
- Does not provide alignment functionality; instead requires external alignment using third-party software such as MAFFT in Geneious and PhyDE.
- Requires manual post-alignment curation.
- Requires manual identification and exclusion of regions with uncertain homology.

ENABLES GENE ALIGNMENT BASED ON AAs:
- No

OTHER FEATURES:
- None.

PLASTIDHUB (ZHANG N.-N. ET AL. 2025)
########################################################################

PROGRAMMING LANGUAGE:
[INFORMATION MISSING!]

GENERAL SOFTWARE STRUCTURE:
- Suite of cloud-based web applications for quadripartite structure standardization, annotation consistency improvement, annotation completeness assessment, feature extraction, molecular marker identification, and gene-level comparative analyses.
- Supports batch processing across all tools.
- Supports modular workflows in which applications can be executed independently or in combination.
- Enables user interaction through static and dynamic pop-up interfaces.

STANDARDIZATION OF GENE NAMES:
- Standardizes plastome structure and annotations.
- Evaluates annotation quality and completeness.
- Identifies and standardizes quadripartite plastome structure, including inverted-repeat positions and lengths.

EXTRACTION OF DNA SEQUENCES:
- Extracts coding and non-coding genomic regions, including protein-coding genes, tRNAs, rRNAs, intron-containing genes, intron-free genes, cis-spliced genes, trans-spliced genes, overlapping genes, non-overlapping genes, nested genes, non-nested genes, single-copy genes, and multi-copy genes.
- Supports standardized extraction of complex trans-spliced genes such as rps12.
- Identifies pseudogenes and redundant genes.
- Reports potentially problematic duplicated entries in 'warning.txt'.
- Reports invalid or problematic codons in 'bad_codons.txt'.

ACCOUNTING FOR GENE DUPLICATION (E.G., IRs):
- Supports batch removal of duplicated sequences across multiple FASTA files.
- Automatically removes entries containing duplicated gene names and identical sequences.
- Performs batch translation of intron-free CDS nucleotide sequences into amino acid sequences. [IMPORTANT: Why not CDS with introns as well?]

ALIGNMENT OF DNA SEQUENCES:
- Performs automated pre-alignment and post-alignment sequence processing.
- Supports automated batch alignment using MAFFT with the predefined strategy '–localpair –maxiterate 16'.
- Batch processing of multiple sequence matrices.
- Restricts alignment to datasets containing at most 50 sequences and sequence lengths of at most 10 kb.
- Records skipped datasets and warning messages in 'warning.txt'.

ENABLES GENE ALIGNMENT BASED ON AAs:
- Assesses codon status of protein-coding genes.

OTHER FEATURES:
- Screens molecular markers based on sequence variation.
- Performs gene-level comparisons of structural variation, annotation completeness, gene number, gene length, and sequence similarity.
- Generates PartitionFinder-compatible alignments.
- Integrates with HomBlocks, ORPA, and OGU.
